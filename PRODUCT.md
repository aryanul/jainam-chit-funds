## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Must host on ordinary shared hosting (e.g. Hostinger).

## Users

Primary: business owners and families in Bengaluru's Chickpet trading district, many from the Jain, Marwari and Gujarati trading communities, aged roughly 30 to 60. They usually arrive on a phone from a WhatsApp forward or a personal referral, already know how an informal family committee works, and are deciding whether a registered chit fund is safer.

Secondary: salaried and self-employed Bengaluru families new to chit funds, who need the mechanism explained plainly before they trust it.

## Product Purpose

Jainam Chit Funds Pvt. Ltd. runs government-registered chit groups. Members pay a fixed monthly instalment; each month an auction decides which member takes the pool; everyone takes it once. The landing page's job is to make a visitor believe their money is legally protected and start a WhatsApp conversation with the founder.

Success: a visitor messages the founder on WhatsApp (directly or by sending a calculated plan).

## Positioning

The family committee people already trust, with the law behind it: registered under the Chit Funds Act, 1982, security deposited with the Registrar of Chits, a legally enforceable written agreement, and a founder who answers personally on WhatsApp instead of a call centre.

Two page variants exist: one that states the Jain founding and Jain values openly, and one that does not mention community.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pools from ₹5 lakh to ₹50 lakh; each group runs 20 months.
- The plan calculator is illustrative only; real amounts depend on each auction and the agreement.
- Undecided/unknown: registration number, payout timeline, the "GST benefit" wording, tax/TDS treatment, founder name and photo, guarantor/KYC specifics. These stay visible placeholders.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Jainam Chit Funds Pvt. Ltd. Logo is a red pot (kalash) with a rupee and hands; the client file is at assets/brand/jainam-logo.png.
- The user rejected: italic or thin serif accent type, and cartoonish 3D renders (a 3D kalash render, a marble look). The approved hero is a photoreal brass kalash loop with coins dropping in.
- Standing preference (user, 2026-11-25): the site must look like a professional, trustworthy finance company, at the level of established chit companies (Shriram Chits, Margadarsi, Kapil Chits) and calm modern finance brands (Groww, Zerodha). No cartoonish 3D, emoji-style renders or gimmick concepts; credibility over novelty.

## Product Principles

1. Safety is proven with specifics (the Act, the Registrar, the agreement), never asserted with adjectives.
2. Explain the mechanism so a first-timer gets it in one look; don't lecture people who already run committees.
3. Every path ends in one human conversation with the founder on WhatsApp.
4. Phone first: most visitors arrive from WhatsApp on a mid-range Android phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience skews 30 to 60 on phones: large readable text, high contrast, 44px+ tap targets, no motion required to understand anything.
