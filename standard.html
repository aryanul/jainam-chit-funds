How a chit fund works

A simple, time-tested way to save and to raise money, within a registered group.

  1. Join a group

    Choose a chit value. You join 19 other members for 20 months.

  2. Pay monthly

    Every member pays the same instalment into the pool each month.

  3. Monthly auction

    Members who need money bid. The member who accepts the biggest discount receives that month's pool.

  4. Everyone receives once

    Over 20 months, every member receives the pool exactly once. The discount is shared as a dividend.

Example, ₹10 lakh chit: you pay up to ₹50,000 a month for 20 months. Need money in month 6? Bid in that month's auction and receive the pool less your discount. Members who wait earn dividends along the way.

Frequently asked questions

Is my money safe with Jainam?

Jainam is registered under the Chit Funds Act, 1982. Security for every group is deposited with the Registrar of Chits, and your chit agreement is enforceable by law. Before joining any chit company, check that it is registered.

How is a chit different from an FD or RD?

An FD or RD pays interest on your own savings. A chit lets you receive the whole pool early, in the month you need it, and pays dividends to members who wait.

Do I keep paying after I receive the pool?

Yes, until the group ends in month 20. As the Act requires, we take security from a member before paying out the pool, so the rest of the group stays protected.
